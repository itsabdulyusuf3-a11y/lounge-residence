Frequently Asked

Is there constant electricity and water?

Yes — the residence runs on full 24/7 power backup with dedicated water supply and hot water in every unit.

How do I pay?

Request on WhatsApp first — once we confirm your residence is available, you pay via bank transfer or card at the ground‑floor office. You never pay before confirmation.

Do you offer airport pickup?

Complimentary pickup from Aminu Kano International Airport on stays of 3+ nights; otherwise a concierge transfer is available at a small fee.

Can I host an event in The Lounge?

Yes — the 4th‑floor Lounge hosts private dinners, proposals and corporate gatherings up to 50 guests from ₦250,000 per event.

Is the residence family friendly?

Absolutely. Two and Three‑Bedroom Residences sleep up to 6, and the residence is gated with 24/7 security and CCTV.
